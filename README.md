# Truth in Food Scanner&#x20;

Truth in Food Scanner is an AI-powered application that helps users understand food ingredients, nutritional information, and product labels using barcode scanning and OCR technology.

## Features

- **Barcode Scanning** – Identify food products by scanning their barcodes.
- **OCR Technology** – Extract text from food packaging and ingredient labels.
- **Ingredient Analysis** – Help users understand ingredients listed on food products.
- **Nutrition Information** – Display available nutritional details.
- **Scan History** – Keep track of previously scanned products.
- **User-Friendly Interface** – Simple and responsive design for easy access to food information.

## How to Use

1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Scan a food product barcode or use the available label-scanning feature.
3. Extract product information using OCR, where supported.
4. Review the available ingredients and nutritional details.
5. View previously scanned products if scan history is implemented.

## Technologies Used

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript
- **OCR:** Optical Character Recognition
- **Barcode Scanning:** Product identification
- **Backend:** Product history and data management, if configured

## Objective

To promote food transparency, improve nutritional awareness, and help users make healthier and more informed food choices.

## Future Enhancements

- AI-based ingredient analysis
- Allergen detection and alerts
- Personalized dietary recommendations
- Expanded food product database
- Nutritional comparison between products

## Note

The availability and accuracy of product information depend on the implemented features and the data sources used by the application.
